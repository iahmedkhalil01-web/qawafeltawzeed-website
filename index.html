About Qawafel Al-Tawzeedعن شركة قوافل التزويد

Qawafel Al-Tawzeed is a Saudi company based in Madinah, focused on transportation services and practical support for business logistics activities. Our approach is built on dependable operations, professional communication, and service consistency.
